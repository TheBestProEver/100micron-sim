## 0. What the design gets right (verified against the code)

* Kernel reading: strict `>` clips; at zero relative motion and constant `Fncrit` nothing changes; `sg = sign(mt)` with `mt` the twist RATE, so an over-cap twist at exactly zero rate is reset to 0 and at an opposite-sign noise rate it FLIPS to the opposite cap (dem.py 376-380). `statics._eval` passes `vi = dU/tau`, so `state()` at `dU = 0` indeed reads an over-cap twist as 0 (latent today, confirmed). LAMMPS's `GranSubModTwistingSDS` uses the same rate sign, so the quirk is LAMMPS-conformant.
* `apply_displacement -> _advance` is exactly one return-map step of the production kernel for a finite displacement (increments `v*tau = dU`, dashpots `g*dU/tau`, ~1e-17 N, negligible but included in the clipped history as the kernel does).
* Lifting the caps (`P_MUT = P_MUR = P_MUTW` huge) leaves the kernel bit-identical whenever no clip branch is taken; `mu` enters only through the three caps. (`Fncrit = |Fne + 2 Fpull| >= Fc > 0` on the stable JKR branch, so `1e300 * Fncrit` never yields 0 or NaN.)
* The elastic-predictor argument is sound: if the lifted-cap equilibrium path keeps every sds demand within its cap, the real kernel never clips, so the plastic variables are exactly unchanged. The quasi-convexity argument is sound for the SECANT between two exact equilibria (`||a + t b|| / (c + t d)` has convex sublevel sets).
* The inventory of places that take ASLEEP as `s = 0` is complete: engine.py 550 (`s`), 671 (`s_full`), 896 (`_early_exit` FREE/ASLEEP branch), 1135/1143 (`_capture_times`), 1383 (`_watch`), 1799 (`_slv_obstacles`); 587 (`_speed_bounds` mask) needs no change because woken members get local bounds in `_early_exit`.
* `_snapshot` shares island dicts (`dict(self._islands)`), so "replace, never mutate" plus `_isl_pending` discarded on redo is the right redo discipline; `n_events` is snapshotted so counters incremented inside `_step_once` are not double counted on redo.
* Load structure: `Omega` piecewise linear (`Omega^2` quadratic, curvature `2 Omega_dot^2 r`), thrust/coast constant, descent table piecewise linear with knots in `_t`, half-sines on `[chute_s, +open]`, `[splash_s, +stop]` with their ends in `breakpoints`; releases and vibration are the only `fast_window` loads.

## 1. Findings (most severe first). Each: the defect, a concrete scenario, the fix.

### A. The stated bound can be exceeded (class 1)

**A1. The E3 ratchet bound "plastic work <= available energy" is false; it is low by 1e2-1e5 exactly for the islands this design targets.** At a capped spring the external load does the work during an outward excursion, not the residual energy. Two-spring toy (what a pile grain is: one spring at its cap, the rest of the structure with released stiffness `k_rel`): the mass starts at equilibrium with kinetic energy `E` moving outward; the capped spring holds constant force `c` = its share of the load, the rest restores with `k_rel`, so the excursion is `a = sqrt(2E/k_rel)` and the whole excursion is permanent slip (the capped spring's elastic part stays at `c/k`). The slider dissipates `c*a`, supplied by `F*a` of the load; the design's bound `E/c` is smaller by `c*sqrt(2/(k_rel E))`. Numbers: `E_res = 1.3e-25 J` (the design's 57-grain pile), `k_rel = k_r = 1.9 N/m`: true slip 3.7e-13 m vs the design's 4e-18 m (ratio 9e4); `E_res = 5e-21 J`: true 7.3e-11 m = 4.2e-3 xi_crit, which VIOLATES the design's own 1e-3 xi_cap tolerance while the design's test (1.5e-13 m) passes it. The scenario is the measured one: a pile in the burn whose rolling springs are the structure (a pile of spheres without rolling resistance is a mechanism, so its soft modes have rolling-spring stiffness). Fix: bound the slip by the residual's displacement amplitude in the RELEASED system: `K_rel = K_el - sum_k (1/k_k) J_k^T fhat_k fhat_k^T J_k` over every sds component within `D` of its cap (`fhat_k` its force direction, a sign for twist; rank-1 each), `dl_rel = statics.energy_bounds(K_rel, J_el, E_res)` (returns None if not positive definite = mechanism: refuse), slip bound `dl_rel,k / k_k + g_k v_E / k_k <= island_ratchet_rel * xi_cap,k`. `E_res = Ek + du^T K_el du / 2 + |dF_step| |K_el^-1 dF_step|` stays as the energy (lifted K over-estimates the stored energy, so it is an upper bound). The same `K_rel` is the consistent tangent of C1. The design's worked figure ("1e-17 m for the 57-grain pile") becomes ~4e-13 m: still accepted, but by a bound that is actually one.

**A2. Step-end and skip-sample loads at a breakpoint are the POST-jump values, so an at-cap island certified up to a breakpoint is committed at the post-jump equilibrium and the jump's transient is dropped.** `_body_force -> Loads.accel(T) -> profile_accel -> apparent_accel -> axial_g` uses half-open pieces `[start, end)`, and `omega_dot` uses `searchsorted(side="right")`: at `T = burn end` or `T = despin_s` both return the next piece. `_outer_dt` ends steps exactly at breakpoints, so `F(T1)` at such a step is post-jump; in `_skip_asleep` the last sample can land on the breakpoint too. The design's `_island_advance(T0, T1)` then solves to the post-jump load and COMMITS that state; at the next step `fast` wakes the island "at the committed state, at rest" = already relaxed to the new load, no transient. The all-DEM applies the step load to the pre-jump state and overshoots: a step `dF` on a mode of stiffness `k_rel` puts `dF^2/(2 k_rel)` into it; at de-spin onset the Euler force jumps by `m * 33 rad/s^2 * 0.1 m = 3.9e-9 N` = 0.12 of a rolling cap, energy 4e-18 J, excursion `sqrt(2E/k_r)` = 2.8e-9 m = 0.16 xi_crit of permanent slip for a spring at its cap. The present 0.90/0.98 islands are far from caps so this was harmless; at-cap islands are not. (Large jumps, e.g. ignition, fail the certificate at `T1` and wake before: fine.) Fix: evaluate step-end loads as LEFT limits: `Loads.accel(T, side="-")` must also apply to the profile (evaluate `apparent_accel` at `nextafter(T, -inf)` when `T` is a breakpoint, or give `axial_g`/`omega_dot` a `side` argument); `_island_load(mem, T, side)`; `_skip_asleep` samples with `side="-"` (as `accel_seg` already does for step ends). Then the committed state at a breakpoint is the pre-jump equilibrium and the DEM resolves the jump from it (the 5e-5 s post-jump step already exists). Add the test E4 below.

**A3. The "kernel nonlinearity" term `2 |lam_1 - (lam_ref + J du)|` is not what the secant argument needs, and the dropped mid-sample was the clean measurement.** The argument requires the exact path's deviation from the secant between the exact endpoints, `<= l^2/8 max|lam''|`. With a fresh tangent, `|lam_1 - lam_lin,1| ~ |lam''| l^2 / 2`, so doubling it is 8x conservative: fine. With K/J refreshed only every 1000 steps or 1e-11 m of drift, the JKR tangent is stale by ~`1e-11 / 3e-9` = 0.3%, a FIRST-order error ~100x the curvature term at these drifts; the measured difference is then dominated by staleness (loose, not unsafe, except by cancellation). Fix (either): (a) one exact mid-chord Newton solve (1-2 iterations from `du0/2`) and take the nonlinearity term as `2 |lam_mid - (lam_ref + lam_1)/2|` (the midpoint-to-secant deviation IS `l^2/8 lam''` for a quadratic; the factor 2 covers non-quadratic), ~0.3 ms more per step; or (b) refresh K/J every step for any island with a component within `1 - margin` of its cap and keep the current term. State the argument in the docstring as: exact endpoints, secant quasi-convexity, path-to-secant curvature bound.

**A4. Kink curvature term is off by 2x.** For a slope jump `s` inside a step of length `l` the maximum chord deviation is `s l / 4` (kink at mid-step), not `l^2/8 * (s/l) = s l / 8`. Fix: `accel_curv` returns the chord deviation directly per axis: `l^2/8 * |a''|` for smooth pieces, `|Delta slope| * l / 4` for each knot in `knots_in` and each spin knot (`Omega_dot` jump) inside the step.

**A5. Units mixed in `tol = island_tol_rel * min_k cap_k`.** Residual `r` holds forces (translational DOFs) and torques (rotational DOFs); caps are N (sliding, rolling) and N m (twist). Fix: per-DOF tolerances: `|r_trans|_inf <= 1e-6 min(force caps)`, `|r_rot|_inf <= 1e-6 min(Mt_crit, R * Fr_crit)`. (`rn_k ||r||_2` is a valid Cauchy-Schwarz bound in any consistent scaling; only the tolerance needs splitting.)

**A6. The tier-1 tolerance `1 + 1e-6` and the tier-2 per-step tolerances are consumed per step with no cumulative budget, so the REPORTED bound is unbounded a priori.** Scenario: tumbling descent (`load_direction` rotating at `tumble_hz`), a corner cluster whose worst rolling ratio oscillates through 1.0000005 every step for 1e5 steps: 0.1 xi_cap of unaccounted flow, and the design's "exact in the plastic variables" claim is false for that island. Fix: `bnd_total_k = flow_tol + ratchet + path + curv (+ snap)` per island; wake with `island_wake_budget` when `max_k bnd_total_k / xi_cap,k > island_budget_rel` (default 1e-2); the budget restarts at re-entry (which re-places the cluster exactly). Report `island_bound_xi` as now.

**A7. `_F_lr` jumps between steps.** `_island_load` adds `self._F_lr`, updated once per step by `_update_long_range`; the committed state is the equilibrium of the previous step's `F_lr`. The Newton residual absorbs it, but `w0` (the start ratio) is evaluated at a state that is not the equilibrium of `F(T0)` with the new `F_lr`. Fix: add `rn_k ||Delta F_lr||` to `D` (or treat `Delta F_lr` as a load jump at `T0` and certify both). Negligible with `contact_charging` off; cheap to include.

**A8. "Exact" needs one more caveat.** The lifted one-shot `apply_displacement` rotates sliding/rolling histories by the net normal rotation (`_rot_rescale`), which differs from the incremental rotation at `O(theta^2)`: `(1e-10 m / 5e-5 m)^2` = 4e-12 relative per step. Document it under tier 1's approximations.

### B. Waking too late (class 2)

**B1. Contact FORMATION inside a drifting island is undetected.** The island's `ci, cj` are the touching contacts at entry; non-touching member pairs (and member-wall pairs) with gaps of 1e-10..1e-8 m exist in a 57-grain pile. Static islands could not close a gap; a creeping one can (1e-10 m per step, 3e-7 m over a burn). The kernel would then form a contact in the DEM (impact record, new stiffness, new cap) while the island's solve knows nothing. Fix: include every member pair and member-wall pair with gap `<= island_gap_m` (e.g. `2 d_cap`) in the island system as inactive contacts (`H_TOUCH = 0`; the kernel returns zero force for `delta < 0`); after each solve, any `hn[:, H_TOUCH]` turning on or any inactive gap `<= du_max` wakes `island_wake_newcontact`. In `solve_equilibrium`, test breaking by `H_TOUCH` transitions (on -> off), not `out[:,10] < 0.5` (which is also 0 for inactive pairs).

**B2. `_watch` join band and the mid-step drift bound.** With `lim = gap - 2 d_cap - s_isl` but the join test still `gap <= 2 d_cap`, a DEM grain at `gap` in `[2 d_cap, 2 d_cap + s_isl]` gets `lim <= 0`, `run_steps` stops after one step, and the DEM crawls one step per contact-list rebuild across the band. Fix: join when `gap - s_isl[j] <= 2 d_cap` (equivalently shrink the committed gap by the drift). Also `s_isl = du_max` is the END displacement; the mid-step placement (`_island_advance(T_ref, T_w)`) or the quasi-static path can exceed it slightly: use `s_isl = du_max + curv_m + 1e-12` (or `2 du_max + 1e-12`) in every bound, and assert in `_wake_island` (mid-step) that `|x_placed - x_committed|_inf <= s_isl` (redo the step with the island forced into the DEM if not: it is the same kind of check as `_verify`'s speed bound).

**B3. The twist-ambiguity hand-off puts the contact into a DEM whose own answer is noise, and it chatters.** For `h > 0` at the cap with `Fncrit` shrinking and a round-off rate `mt < 0`, the kernel sets `h = -Mtcrit/k`, torque `+Mtcrit` (a jump of `2 Mtcrit`), the grain starts twisting, the rate turns positive, next step `h = +Mtcrit/k`: a limit cycle at the DEM step. Cap shrink in the early coast is only 0.04% of `Fncrit` (2 g -> 0.2 g against `2 Fc`), so this affects only twists within 4e-4 of their cap, but each such edge grain then costs 1e8 DEM steps per flight-second for the ~2 s of decay. This is the status quo and the design is right not to pretend otherwise; but say so in the risks with the cost, and offer a documented, OFF-by-default option `twist_continuity` that resolves the zero-rate case in the creep return map toward continuity (`h_new = sign(h_ref) * Mtcrit_new / k_tw`), verified in check 14(b) against the DEM's envelope (the DEM's answer there is `+-Mtcrit` chatter; the continuity answer is its upper envelope). Mark it ASSUMED in the provenance if ever switched on.

**B4. Yield test wakes already-yielded contacts.** The kernel re-yields only for `delta > P_DY and delta >= H_DMAX (1 - 1e-12)`; `overlaps <= P_DY` wakes a flattened contact unloading elastically on its plastic branch. Fix: wake on `(hn[:, H_YLD] > 0.5) & (h_ref[:, H_YLD] < 0.5)` or on `hn[:, H_DMAX] > max(P_DY, h_ref[:, H_DMAX])`: the kernel already did the test in `apply_displacement`.

### C. The measured cases may never sleep (class 3)

**C1. The K_el fixed-point iteration stalls for exactly the measured piles.** Its contraction factor is released/total stiffness in the released direction (the design says so). A pile of spheres is held against its rotational collapse modes by rolling and twisting springs only (sliding springs do not resist relative rotation at point contacts), so when "most" of a 57-grain pile's rolling springs are at their caps the released fraction of its soft modes is ~1 and `q -> 1`: `n_max = 40` is not reached, `island_wake_newton` fires every step, and the burns stay at ~4e5 DEM steps x 57 grains per step: the headline speed-up for the burns and the early coast does not materialise. Design 3's active-set Newton was dismissed as "more than needed"; it is needed. Fix: a consistent elasto-plastic tangent `K_c = K_el - sum_{capped k} (1/k_k) J_k^T fhat_k fhat_k^T J_k` (rank-1 per capped component, `fhat_k` from the return-mapped trial force; the clipped vector springs keep their rotational stiffness `cap/|f_trial| * k (I - fhat fhat^T)` ~ the elastic one), refactorised (sparse `splu`, ~ms at 150 contacts) whenever the capped set changes, or applied by Woodbury on the existing `lu`. Newton with `K_c` converges quadratically off the kinks; `splu(K_c)` failing or `energy_bounds(K_c, ...)` None = a true mechanism = DEM (`island_wake_mechanism`). Keep the K_el fixed point only as the first iterate. This `K_c` is also A1's `K_rel`.

**C2. `island_motion_m = 1e-10 m` is within a factor 1-5 of the expected creep.** Relative centrifugal growth per 4 ms step is `2H/T`: 4e-4 at T+20 s, 1.6e-3 at T+5 s; on an 8 g lateral load (9e-8 N on a 50 um grain) that is 3.6e-11 N per step, a displacement of 1.9e-11 m at rolling-spring stiffness (8e-11 m at T+5 s). Grains on top of a stack are softer still. Expect frequent `island_wake_motion` early in the burns. Fix: with `s_isl` plumbed everywhere (B2), the limit can be `1e-9 m` (1e-2 d_cap; capture still happens at `2 d_cap`); make it the default and report the per-step `du_max` distribution in the campaign diagnostics.

**C3. A tier-1 candidate failing the 20-step drift test is refused instead of placed as tier 2.** In the coast's cap shrink every component at 0.9999 fails the extrapolation; those islands ARE about to flow and belong in tier 2. Fix: `cluster_awake_drift` only when `creep_islands` is off; else tier 2.

**C4. Approach wakes are not quantified.** A mover (1e-4 m/s) within `2 d_cap` of a pile wakes it every step (`_watch`), and a mover touching the pile makes it one cluster (not static: refused). The speed-up estimate assumes the static piles are isolated. Fix: none in the engine; the campaign diagnostic in the verification list must read `island_wake_approach` and the sizes of refused clusters on the campaign's autosaves (29 Oct 2026) before the speed-up is claimed.

**C5. E3 applied to every sds component** including sliding springs at 1% of their cap (whose `xi_cap = 8e-11 m` makes the tolerance 8e-14 m): pointless refusals are possible. Fix: apply the ratchet bound only to components within `D + dl_rel` of their cap; the others cannot flow by construction.

### D. Existing machinery (class 4)

**D1. `cluster_awake_certificate` after placement silently alters a refused cluster's DEM state** (moved to the equilibrium, `v = w = 0`): an unverified perturbation of a cluster that stays in the DEM, which `_verify` cannot see. Fix: make `_island_advance` pure (operates on candidate arrays `x_c, h_c`, not `self.x/self._hist`); run the first certificate on the candidate; write only on acceptance.

**D2. Counters double counted** by the entry certificate (`island_static_steps`, `island_newton_iters`): pass `count=False` for the entry call.

**D3. The regression claim "tier-0 islands are bit-identical" is false.** Entry placement changes from one linear `apply_displacement(du)` to a Newton solve, and committed states drift; the kernel EVALUATION is bit-identical, the island state is not. Checks 10, 11, 13 and the tests pass by criteria, not bitwise; `test_killed_tubule_run_continues_from_its_autosave` and `test_checkpoint_with_sleeping_cluster_is_exact` are within one code version and stay valid. Fix the wording in the design, README and CHECKS.md.

**D4. Test count and runtime.** `tests/test_full_engine.py::test_full_engine_check` is parametrised over `verify_full.CHECKS`; appending checks 14-17 adds four tests with minutes of all-DEM each (30 ms at 5-18 ns for several grains at n_ppp 50 and 100). State the budget (or mark them slow) so "98 tests unchanged" is replaced by "102+ tests, N minutes".

**D5. `_record_break` in the mid-step fallback** (`apply_displacement` with the real prm) writes `H_TBREAK = 0.0` and increments `H_NBREAK` into a history that is committed to `self._hist`, so `_log_events` would log a break at t = -t0_flight. Pre-existing in `_wake_island`; the design's `broke -> wake` path avoids it only in the solve branch. Fix: in the fallback, if any contact broke, discard `hn` and hand the committed state itself to the DEM (which resolves the break with the right time stamp).

**D6. Pull-off entry at `1 - margin` with no hysteresis** (the 20-step extrapolation is stated for sds ratios only): a ceiling grain whose pull-off ratio creeps through 0.98 under the growing centrifugal pull chatters sleep/wake every step. Fix: apply the 20-step extrapolation to the pull-off ratio too (threshold `1 - margin`).

**D7. `_skip_asleep` lockstep commits immediately** while `_update_long_range` is not called during a skip (pre-existing): fine, but say that `F_lr` is frozen during skips in the docstring (A7 covers the jump at the next step).

**D8. Old pickles:** `_island_upgrade` must also add the inactive near pairs of B1 and the `tier`/`bnd` fields; islands upgraded from the old one-linear-step placement are not exact equilibria: the first Newton absorbs it (fine), but `bnd.ratchet_m` is unknown for them: set it to the E3 bound recomputed at upgrade (their `v = w = 0`, so `E_res = du^T K du / 2` of the first Newton step).

### E. Testability (class 5)

**E1.** Check 14(a) "histories unchanged to 1e-12": the all-DEM's capped histories are noisy (round-off clipping), so state it as: the ENGINE's committed histories bit-identical over the 30 ms; the DEM's within `3x` its own n_ppp 50 vs 100 spread.
**E2.** Check 16 must assert convergence (`island_newton_iters / island_creep_steps <= 10`) and include a true mechanism: a 2-grain stack whose two rolling springs are both capped must wake (`island_wake_mechanism`) and the all-DEM must show it rolling.
**E3.** New unit test for A1: an edge grain (floor + side wall) with its floor rolling spring at the cap and a residual oscillation of prescribed energy in the all-DEM: measured permanent slip vs the released-ellipsoid bound (must hold) and vs `E/cap` (must fail): this is the test that would have caught A1.
**E4.** New test for A2: an island certified through to a breakpoint (the de-spin knot) must be committed at the LEFT-limit equilibrium; the engine across the knot vs all-DEM across the knot (the transient resolved by the DEM) within 3x step error.
**E5.** Check 17 must include a descent-table knot and the de-spin knot (slope jumps), not only smooth pieces, and assert the kink term (A4).

## 2. Corrected criteria (summary)

**Entry** (`_try_sleep_island`, pure on candidate arrays): E0 no fast window or breakpoint in `[T, T+H]`, size `<= max_island`, `K_el` factorises; island system = touching contacts PLUS member pairs/walls with gap `<= 2 d_cap` as inactive contacts. E1 lifted Newton from the DEM state to `F(T)` (per-DOF tolerances 1e-6 of the force / torque caps): if every sds ratio `<= 1 + 1e-6`, tier 1; else, with `creep_islands`, the real-kernel return-map Newton with the consistent tangent `K_c` (`<= 40` iterations), tier 2; `splu(K_c)` failing = mechanism: refuse. E2 pull-off `<= 1 - margin` at `T` and `T+H` (left limit) including `dl_rel`, no yield (`H_YLD`/`H_DMAX` unchanged), no break, no new contact. E3 residual `E_res = Ek + du^T K_el du/2 + |dF_step||K_el^-1 dF_step|`; released ellipsoid `dl_rel = energy_bounds(K_rel, J_el, E_res)` (None: refuse); for every sds component within `D + dl_rel` of its cap, `dl_rel,k/k_k + g_k v_E/k_k <= 1e-3 xi_cap,k`; `_linres` only when no sds component is within `1 - margin` of its cap. E4 first certificate on the candidate; 20-step linear extrapolation of sds AND pull-off ratios (`<= 1 + 1e-6`, `<= 1 - margin`) for tier 1, else tier 2 (refuse only with creep off); then place (snapped, at rest). Single grains on one wall: entry = stay at `1 - margin` with the chord-deviation term and the 20-step extrapolation; one contact at its cap is a mechanism: ROLL.

**Stay** (`_step_once`, `_skip_asleep` in lockstep, loads at step ends as LEFT limits): from the committed state (exact equilibrium of `F(T0)`), lifted Newton to `F(T1)` and to `F(T_mid)`; `D_k = rn_k (||dF_chord|| + ||r|| + ||f_dyn|| + ||Delta F_lr||) + 2 |lam_mid - (lam_ref + lam_1)/2|_k`, with `dF_chord` the per-axis chord deviation (`l^2/8 |a''|` smooth, `|Delta slope| l/4` at knots, `2 Omega_dot^2 r l^2/8`), `f_dyn = m|du|/l^2 + g_max|du|/l + 2 m Omega |du|/l` (+ rotational analogue). Tier 1: `max(margins_bounded(lam_ref, D), margins_bounded(lam_1, D))` sds `<= 1 + 1e-6`, pull-off `<= 1 - margin`, no yield/break/new contact, `max_k bnd_total_k/xi_cap,k <= 1e-2`. Else tier 2 (sds only exceeded, creep on): `K_c` Newton to `F(T1)` and the half-step pair; `|du|_inf <= 1e-9 m`; plastic increment `<= 0.05 xi_cap`; twist increments not rate-ambiguous (or `twist_continuity` on); pull-off/yield/break/new contact as above; path error `2|h_1step - h_2step| <= 1e-3 xi_cap`; budget as above. Pending states committed after `_verify` (replace the dict), immediately in `_skip_asleep`; `s_isl = du_max + curv_m + 1e-12` enters `_watch` (lim and join), `_capture_times`, `_early_exit`, `_slv_obstacles`.

**Wake** (reason counted): fast, breakpoint, pulloff, break, yield, newcontact, mechanism (no `K_c` factor / no convergence), loading (creep off), motion, flow, twist, path, budget, approach, fallback. Placement: at the step start the committed (pre-jump) state at rest (+ linres residual); mid-step the solve from the committed state to `F(T_w)`, asserting `|dx| <= s_isl`, fallback to the linear placement (counted; discard `hn` if it broke a contact).

**Bounds reported** per island and in `n_events`: `island_bound_xi` (= `max_k bnd_total_k / xi_cap,k`, enforced by the budget), `island_flow_m`, `island_snap_rel`, `island_wake_transient_m`.

## 3. Verdict

The architecture (elastic certificate with lifted caps, return-map creep, pending/commit, left-to-right verification) is right and testable. As written, two parts would fail the project's standard: the E3 ratchet bound is not a bound (A1), and loads at breakpoints are right-continuous, so at-cap islands would be committed past a jump and the DEM transient dropped (A2). One part would defeat the purpose: the K_el fixed point stalls on the capped piles (C1). The remaining findings are plumbing (B1, B2, D1) and wording (D3, A8). With the fixes above the corrected criteria in section 2 are exact in the plastic variables for tier 1 and bounded, with an enforced cumulative budget, for tier 2.

## Stay and wake (summary)

Stay (every outer step in _step_once; every dts sample in _skip_asleep, islands in lockstep; all step-end / sample loads as LEFT limits so a step ending at a breakpoint is certified against the pre-jump load): from the committed state (exact equilibrium of F(T0), positions/histories in self.x/self._hist) one lifted-cap Newton solve to F(T1) and one to F(T_mid) (1-2 iterations from du0/2); deviation bound D_k = rn_k (||dF_chord|| + ||r|| + ||f_dyn|| + ||Delta F_lr||) + 2 |lam_mid - (lam_ref + lam_1)/2|_k, where rn = row norms of J K_el^-1, dF_chord the per-axis chord deviation of the loads over l = T1 - T0 (l^2/8 |a''| on smooth pieces, |Delta slope| l/4 at knots of the profile and of Omega, 2 Omega_dot^2 r l^2/8 for the centrifugal term), f_dyn = m|du|/l^2 + g_max|du|/l + 2 m Omega |du|/l (and the rotational analogue), Delta F_lr the long-range force change since the last step; worst = max(margins_bounded(lam_ref, D), margins_bounded(lam_1, D)) (secant between exact equilibria, quasi-convex, so the ends bound the step). Tier 1 (static, exact in the plastic variables): every sds component <= 1 + 1e-6, pull-off <= 1 - margin, no yield (H_YLD/H_DMAX unchanged), no break, no inactive contact forming, cumulative bound max_k bnd_total_k/xi_cap,k <= island_budget_rel (1e-2) -> pending end state, du_max = |du|_inf. Else, if only sds caps are exceeded and creep_islands: real-kernel return-map Newton with the consistent tangent K_c to F(T1) plus the half-step pair (T0 -> Tmid -> T1): converged (<= 40 it; K_c singular = mechanism), |du|_inf <= island_motion_m (1e-9 m), plastic increment <= 5% xi_cap per component, no twist with zero/opposite-sign increment over its cap (unless twist_continuity is on, then resolved toward continuity and counted), pull-off/yield/break/new-contact as above, path error 2|h_1step - h_2step| <= 1e-3 xi_cap, budget as above -> pending = the half-step state (tier 2), bounds accumulated (flow_tol, ratchet, path, curv, snap). Pending states are committed in step() after _verify passes (replace the dict; write x/hist), immediately in _skip_asleep; a redo discards them. The DEM sees the island at its committed start state with s_isl = du_max + curv_m + 1e-12 in every bound (_watch: lim and the join test gap - s_isl <= 2 d_cap; _capture_times; _early_exit; _slv_obstacles). ## What is exact and what is bounded (summary)

Tier 1 (no predicted flow) is exact in the plastic variables: while the bounded ratio stays <= 1 at every sliding/rolling/twisting component the kernel takes no clip branch, so the committed state equals the DEM's quasi-static state up to (i) the Newton residual, effect <= rn_k ||r|| <= 1e-6 cap per step, not cumulative; (ii) the neglected dynamics of the quasi-static drift, <= rn_k ||m du/l^2 + g_max du/l + 2 m Omega du/l|| (~1e-9 cap at 1e-13 m per step; required <= 1e-6); (iii) the tolerated excess <= 1e-6 cap/k per step, summed into the budget, zero when the prediction is <= 1; (iv) snapping to (1 - 2^-40) cap, <= 9.1e-13 relative per snap; (v) the one-shot vs incremental rotation of vector histories, O((du/R)^2) ~ 4e-12 relative per step; (vi) the dropped entry residual, bounded by the residual's displacement amplitude in the RELEASED system: slip_k <= dl_rel,k/k_k + g_k v_E/k_k with dl_rel = energy_bounds(K_rel, J_el, E_res) (the two-spring derivation: the capped spring holds its cap while the rest of the structure restores with the released stiffness, so the excursion sqrt(2E/k_rel) is permanent slip; the load, not the residual energy, does the plastic work), required <= 1e-3 xi_cap, one-time per entry; for the 57-grain pile at 2e-9 m/s (E_res ~ 1.3e-25 J, k_rel ~ k_r = 1.9 N/m) this is ~4e-13 m = 2e-5 xi_crit (the superseded E/cap bound gave 4e-18 m, low by 9e4; at E_res = 5e-21 J it would have accepted 4e-3 xi_crit of slip). The between-sample inputs (chord deviation of the loads: 0 on constant and linear pieces, |Delta slope| l/4 at knots, l^2/8 |a''| on the smooth ascent drag and the tumbling direction with accel_bound's doubled second-difference heuristic, exact omega^2 A l^2/8 on the half-sines; the path's deviation from the secant measured at the exact mid-chord state and doubled) are certificate conditions, verified by check 17 on smooth pieces, knots and the de-spin kink. Tier 2 (creep) is bounded: flow amounts are the kernel's own return map at the consistent-tangent equilibrium; one-shot-vs-continuous return and in-step path dependence are estimated per step by the half-step pair e_k = 2|h_1step - h_2step|_k <= 1e-3 xi_cap (first-order Richardson; expected ~1e-5 xi_cap per step at 1e-12 m drifts), summed and reported; load-chord flow rn ||dF_chord||/k per step summed; dynamics and Newton as in tier 1; |du| <= 1e-9 m per step enters every approach bound exactly as s_isl = du_max + curv_m + 1e-12. Enforced total: the island wakes (island_wake_budget) when max_k (flow_tol + ratchet + path + curv + snap)_k / xi_cap,k > 1e-2, so the reported island_bound_xi is never above 1e-2 xi_cap for a sleeping island; the budget restarts at each exact re-entry. Loads at step ends are left limits, so no committed state ever sits past a load jump: the DEM resolves every breakpoint transient from the pre-jump equilibrium (the de-spin Euler jump, 0.12 of a rolling cap, could otherwise ratchet a capped spring by up to 0.16 xi_crit unaccounted). Reported per island and in n_events: island_bound_xi, island_flow_m, island_snap_rel, island_wake_transient_m (the DEM's measured motion of woken grains). Worst allowed per island between re-entries: 1e-2 xi_crit by construction; the measured values (check 16) are expected ~1e-3 xi_crit over a 12 s burn.

## Risks stated

- Creep islands are a reduced quasi-static model (like slaved.py); their per-step error is estimated by the half-step pair (Richardson for a first-order return map), not proven; the enforced budget (1e-2 xi_cap per island between re-entries) bounds what is tolerated, and check 16 against the all-DEM at two steps is what certifies it; creep_islands stays off until it passes
- The between-sample chord-deviation term inherits accel_bound's doubled-second-difference heuristic for the smooth ascent drag and the tumbling direction; exact for constant, linear (with the l/4 kink term), half-sine and quadratic (Omega^2) pieces; check 17 measures violations on the real loads including knots
- The consistent tangent K_c is refactorised whenever the capped set changes; for piles alternating capped sets step to step this is one sparse splu per step (~ms at 150 contacts, seconds at 800 grains); above ~128 grains fall back to the present linear 0.98 test; true mechanisms (K_c singular) wake into the DEM each step at the status-quo cost
- The released-ellipsoid ratchet bound assumes the residual decays within the released elastic system (dashpots present, loads changing slowly); a residual large enough to re-cap other springs during its excursion is excluded by requiring the bound to hold at every component within D + dl_rel of its cap, and by refusing when energy_bounds(K_rel) is None
- Islands now drift (s_isl = du_max + curv_m + 1e-12 per step) and their committed positions change every step: every place that took ASLEEP as s = 0 (_watch lim and join, _capture_times, _early_exit, _slv_obstacles) is updated and the mid-step wake asserts |dx| <= s_isl; a missed site would be an unverified ~1e-2 d_cap assumption (the _missed safety net still catches contact formation); contact formation between island members is now watched through inactive near pairs
- The all-DEM reference at a cap is itself noisy (round-off clipping, the twist cap chatters between +-Mtcrit under cap shrink), so verification differences at the ~1e-5 cap level are dominated by the DEM's step noise; the criteria include the step error and the reported bound; the twist_continuity option, if ever enabled, is an ASSUMED resolution of a LAMMPS-conformant ambiguity and must be marked as such
- Loads at step ends become left limits: a behavioural change for every sleeper and island certified up to a breakpoint (they now wake at the pre-jump state and the DEM resolves the jump); more short DEM transients at breakpoints, which the existing 5e-5 s post-jump step already handles
- Campaign autosaves continue under the new rule but are no longer bit-for-bit the uninterrupted old run; tier-0 islands are placed by Newton and drift, so the README statement must be scoped to one code version, and bound accumulators start at the resume point (the ratchet bound is recomputed at upgrade)
- Per-step cost grows by two lifted solves per island (end and mid-chord, ~0.6 ms at 150 contacts) and ~3-4 solves plus an occasional refactorisation for creep islands; the pytest suite grows by six all-DEM checks of minutes each
- If the genuinely moving grains (1e-4 m/s) stay in the DEM at 5-18 ns the coast still costs hundreds of seconds per flight-second, and a mover within 2 d_cap of a pile wakes it every step (island_wake_approach); the speed-up then depends on the slaved/rolling models taking the movers and on the piles being isolated, which the campaign diagnostic must confirm before the speed-up is claimed
