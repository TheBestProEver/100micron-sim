Dust-collision simulator

Simulates tiny dust grains (about the width of a hair) floating in near-zero gravity, like on our NASA RockSat flight, and finds the speed below which they stick together when they collide. Pick the dust, press Run, and watch it in 3D. It all runs in your browser.
