# Design A: elastic-unloading certificate

Verbatim output of one agent of the design run (29 Oct 2026). Only local file paths were replaced by `<repository>`; line numbers refer to the code as it was then (commit e512b2d). Statements about the code are the author's own and were checked by the next stage (see [README.md](README.md)); where a later stage found an error, the later stage is right.

**Title given by the author:** Capped-contact sleep: elastic-unloading certificate with exact per-step re-referencing (tier-1 islands)

## 0. What the code does now and why static piles are refused

`_try_sleep_island` (engine.py 1905) accepts a cluster only if `rr.max() <= 1 - 5*margin = 0.90` at T and T+H, where `rr` comes from `statics.margins(lam_eq)`; the stay test (`_step_once` ~495, `_skip_asleep` 1153) is `_island_ratio <= 0.98` at sampled times from the frozen reference `lam_ref + J K^-1 (F(T) - F_ref)`. A contact whose sds spring sits at its cap has ratio 1 - O(1e-16) (the kernel clips `xr` to `Frcrit/kr` at the last DEM step), so these clusters can never pass. Two further facts from `dem.contact_force` (lines ~300-392) drive the design:

* With zero relative motion the kernel changes a plastic variable (`xt`, `xr`, `xtw`) **only** when its trial force strictly exceeds the cap `mu*Fncrit`, `Fncrit = |Fne + 2 Fpull|`. At ratio <= 1 nothing happens, at exactly 1 nothing happens. Hence the DEM would leave a resting cluster's plastic state untouched as long as every ratio stays <= 1 (not 0.98) along the true load path.
* `statics._eval/_advance/state/_jac_coo` call the real kernel, so (i) central differences at a contact within 1e-13 m*k of its cap straddle the clip and give a wrong (half-elastic) tangent, and (ii) `state()` runs at zero velocity, where an over-cap twist (ratio > 1 by round-off) hits the quirk `sg = 0 -> h[H_XTW] = 0, tq = 0` and reads as **zero** twist load. Both are fixed by evaluating statics with the caps lifted (below); for ratios <= 0.90 the lifted kernel is bit-identical to the real one (the clip branch is never taken), so tier-0 islands are unchanged.

## 1. Criteria

### 1.1 Lifted-cap statics (statics.py)
`elastic_prm(prm)`: copy of `prm` with `P_MUT, P_MUR, P_MUTW = 1e300`. `state`, `jacobians`, `apply_displacement` (and `_eval_v`) use it; `margins()`/`margins_bounded()` keep the real `prm` (they need mu). Effect: `lam` is the elastic spring force `-k*history`, which for a DEM-clipped history equals the cap at the last DEM `Fncrit` (ratio 1 to round-off); K, J are the elastic tangent everywhere; the twist quirk cannot fire inside statics. Add `r_yield = max(Fne,0)/F_y`, `F_y = (4/3)E* sqrt(R) delta_y^1.5` to `margins()` so "no plastic variable changes" includes Thornton-Ning flattening (`H_YLD`).

### 1.2 Tiers
An island is **tier 0** (unchanged: entry 0.90, stay 0.98, frozen reference, optional linres) if `rr.max() <= 1 - 5*margin`. Otherwise it is tried as **tier 1** ("capped island"), size <= `cap_sleep_max_grains` (default 64; the 57-grain pile fits), no `res`/linres (a residual large enough to need linres fails 1.4 anyway).

### 1.3 Tier-1 entry (in `_try_sleep_island`, after the tier-0 test fails)
1. `H > 0`, no fast window, no breakpoint in [T, T+H] (as now).
2. **Exact equilibrium at T**: `statics.newton_equilibrium`: repeat `du = K^-1 (Fext + Fnet)`, `(x,h) <- apply_displacement(du)` (lifted caps), `lam, Fnet, out = state(x,h)`, until `|Fext + Fnet|_inf <= tol_N = cap_newton_tol * min_k cap_k` (default 1e-6), max 4 iterations; recompute K, J, LU at the final state. This replaces the single linear step and makes the reference state's forces **exact kernel values**; the only approximation is the measured residual, whose effect on any ratio is <= `(|J| |K^-1| |r|)_k / cap_k <= 1e-6` (reported).
3. **Cap condition at the samples**: `rho_k(Ts) <= 1 + eps_flow` for Ts = T, T+H/2, T+H, where `rho_k(Ts)` is computed by the same exact procedure on a *copy* (linear prediction `du = K^-1 (F(Ts) - F(T))`, apply, `state`, Newton to tol_N). `eps_flow = cap_flow_tol` (default 1e-6).
4. **Between the samples (interval bound)**: on each half-interval `rho_k <= max(rho_k(Ta), rho_k(Tb)) + kappa_k`. Justification: lam is affine in the load along the chord in the linear response, and the norm of an affine vector divided by an affine positive `Fncrit` is convex along the chord (the perspective image of a segment is a segment), so the linear-response ratio has its maximum at the ends. The two deviations from the chord are (a) the load's own curvature: `Loads.accel_curv(T0,T1)` (the `2*h^2|a''|/8` term factored out of `accel_bound`, zero for the piecewise-linear thrust/spin parts, plus `(dOmega)^2 r/4` for the centrifugal term) mapped to forces `dF = m * curv` and to contact quantities `dlam = |J| (|K^-1| dF)` with the dense `|K^-1|` stored at entry (6n x 6n; 1.2 MB at 64 grains), inserted through `margins_bounded`; (b) the nonlinearity of the exact `rho(F)` between exactly evaluated samples, bounded as the loads module bounds `a''`: `kappa_lin,k = 2 * |rho_k(T) - 2 rho_k(T+H/2) + rho_k(T+H)| / 8` (doubled second difference; documented as the same assumption `accel_bound` makes). Sizes for the coast: drag `a'' ~ 25 g / (10 s)^2` gives `dF ~ 6e-15 N` vs cap `1.3e-8 N` -> `kappa ~ 4e-7`; for linear thrust ramps 0.
5. **Residual motion (ratcheting) at capped contacts**: with `E = Ek + Ep` (as now), `delta_k = sqrt(2E (J Ks^-1 J^T)_kk)` (`statics.energy_bounds`, exact for the ellipsoid; E only decreases, clipping dissipates) **plus the dashpot term** of each cap check: rolling `gr * 2R sqrt(2E/I_min)`, sliding `x_gt*dpre * 2 sqrt(2E/m_min)`, twisting `gtw * 2 sqrt(2E/I_min)`. In the DEM a capped contact can ratchet by at most `Delta_p_k = delta_k / k_k` (one excursion: after the first clip the spring's mean has moved by the excursion and later, smaller excursions no longer exceed the cap). Accept if `delta_k <= cap_ratchet_tol * cap_k` (default 1e-3) for every contact with `rho_k > 1 - 5*margin`, and for the others the existing 1 % rule. `Delta_p_k` is added to the island's `ratchet_bound` (per contact, m) and to `n_events["cap_ratchet_bound_m"]` (max cumulative) and `..._xi_crit`.
6. **Twist-quirk guard**: if any contact has `rho_twist > 1` and its relative twist increment in the applied `du` is exactly 0.0, refuse (the real kernel would zero that twist on waking). Never triggers for rho <= 1.
7. Place the cluster at the Newton state, v = w = 0, store the reference (1.6). Count `cluster_sleep_cap`.

Numbers for the measured cases: 57-grain pile at 2e-9 m/s: `E <= 1.3e-25 J`, rolling-row ellipsoid bound `~4.7e-13 N = 3.6e-5 cap` -> accepted; ratchet bound `5.5e-13 m = 3.5e-5 xi_crit`. Grains at 1e-4 m/s: `~1e-6 N >> cap` -> refused (they really move). Microgravity edge grain (twist 0.962) and 3-grain bridge (roll 0.926): loads ~1e-4 g vary by < 1e-6 of the cap per step, kappa ~ 0 -> accepted.

### 1.4 Stay test each outer step (`_step_once` step 1) and in `_skip_asleep`
Tier 0: unchanged. Tier 1: `_island_predict(isl, T, H)` performs 1.3.3-1.3.4 for the step's samples (exact Newton evaluation on copies; refactorise K/J only if the Newton residual after one iteration exceeds tol_N, counted `cap_relin`). Pass: every `rho_k <= 1 + eps_flow` including kappa. The predicted end state (x, hist, lam, out, F_ref = F(T+H), residual) is kept as `pending`; the island's motion bound for the step is `s[mem] = max |du_pred| per grain` (replacing the present 0, also applied to tier 0 as `|K^-1 dF|`); after `_verify` passes, `step()` commits it (`_island_commit`) before `_classify`; a redo discards it (`_snapshot` keeps the old dict object because `_island_commit` builds a new dict rather than mutating). `_skip_asleep` runs predict+commit per dts sample (consecutive triples give kappa_lin) and stops at the first failure, exactly as it stops now on 0.98. Tolerated excess `max(0, rho_k - 1) * cap_k / k_k` (<= eps_flow cap/k = 1.6e-14 m per step) accumulates in `flow_bound` / `n_events["cap_flow_bound_m"]`; it is zero whenever the prediction is <= 1.

### 1.5 Wake rule
Wake the whole island (into the DEM, as now) when: a fast window or breakpoint is in the step (as now); any `rho_k > 1 + eps_flow` (count `cap_wake_loading`); Newton fails tol_N in 4 iterations (`cap_wake_newton`); the quirk guard fires; a DEM grain comes within 2 d_cap (`_watch`, as now; the sleeper tree is already keyed on positions, so the quasi-static drift of tier-1 islands rebuilds it). `_wake_island`: start from the committed state (exact at T_ref), apply the linear response to the current load with the **real** kernel (any clip there is the flow the DEM would do at that instant, bounded by the excess and counted `cap_wake_flow_m`), v = w = 0.

### 1.6 Island reference (checkpoint/pickle)
New keys, all numpy/float: `tier` (0/1), `rho_ref` (per contact), `near` (bool, rho > 1 - 5 margin), `Kinv_abs` (dense |K^-1| or None), `ratchet_bound`, `flow_bound` (per contact, m), `r_newton`, `n_relin`, `pending` (dropped by `__getstate__` with `lu`). Old islands lack `tier` -> `isl.get("tier", 0)`: existing autosaves/checkpoints load and continue unchanged; `branch()` and `state_signature` are unaffected (no islands at init).

## 2. Functions that change
* **statics.py**: `elastic_prm`; `_eval/_eval_v/_advance/state/jacobians/apply_displacement` take the lifted prm; `margins`/`margins_bounded` + `r_yield`, and a `margins_by_kind` returning (ratio, kind) for diagnostics; new `abs_inverse(lu, n)`, `variation_bound(Kinv_abs, J, dF)`, `residual_cap_bound(K, J, E, prm, m, I)` (ellipsoid + dashpot terms), `newton_equilibrium(...)`.

* **engine.py**: `_try_sleep_island` (tier split, 1.3), new `_island_predict`, `_island_commit`, `_island_certificate` (shared by `_step_once` and `_skip_asleep`), `_island_ratio` (tier 0 only), `_wake_island` (real-kernel placement, flow count), `step()` (commit after verify), `_step_once` (s[mem] for islands), `_skip_asleep` (per-sample predict/commit), `_classify` (a single grain on one wall that fails `_try_sleep` only on the 0.90 test is offered to `_try_sleep_island([k])` before `_try_roll`), `__getstate__` (drop `pending`), new cfg keys `contact.cap_sleep` (True), `cap_flow_tol` 1e-6, `cap_ratchet_tol` 1e-3, `cap_newton_tol` 1e-6, `cap_sleep_max_grains` 64, and the `n_events` keys above.
* Interactions: linres islands stay tier 0; slaved clusters unchanged (`_classify` order kept, fewer slaved entries); `_verify` sees islands with a finite `s` instead of 0; `_watch`/`_dem` unchanged; `all_dem=True` bypasses everything (reference mode).

## 3. Verification
New verify_full checks (criteria in the style of checks 10/13: engine vs all-DEM at the engine's step and half of it; "same contacts; each <= 3x the DEM step error + the reported bound"):
1. `cap_sleep_pile`: 4 silica grains in a floor/wall edge driven to their rolling caps by a lateral ramp in the full DEM, then 20 ms of (a) constant, (b) decreasing (1 -> 0.5 of the limit), (c) increasing load. (a),(b): the engine must sleep the cluster (`cluster_sleep_cap >= 1`, no wake before the end), positions and `xr, xt, xtw` histories within 3x step error + `cap_ratchet_bound_m + cap_flow_bound_m`; the actual |Delta xr| between engine and all-DEM must not exceed the reported bound (the bound is honest). (c): the engine must refuse or wake within one step (`cap_wake_loading >= 1`) and still match all-DEM within 3x step error. Proves no-flow correctness, the unloading certificate, and that creep is never silently frozen.
2. `cap_sleep_edge_twist`: a grain in an edge with locked-in normal forces (0.66 uN) and twist at 0.95 of its cap under 1e-4 g with slowly rotating direction, 50 ms: engine sleeps it (tier 1), twist history identical to all-DEM to 1e-6 relative, position within 3x step error. Proves the microgravity case and that the twist quirk is not triggered.
3. `cap_statics_exact`: `state()` with lifted caps vs the DEM's own forces at the last step for histories at the sliding/rolling/twisting caps: <= 1e-12 relative; the real-kernel `state()` at an over-cap twist returns 0 (documents the quirk the fix avoids).
4. `cap_interval_bound`: 1000 random sub-intervals of the nominal profile (burns, coast, microgravity), a 12-grain pile: exact ratio at 101 interior points <= the bound (max of ends + kappa) in every case; reports the largest slack.
Unit tests: tier-1 entry/refusal with synthetic states; pickle round-trip and resumed run bit-identical with a tier-1 island; redo (`_snapshot/_restore`) leaves a tier-1 island and its pending state untouched; island dict without `tier` loads as tier 0; `_skip_asleep` advances tier-1 islands and stops at a certificate failure; `margins()` r_yield.

## 4. Speed-up and risks
Microgravity (800 s): all three refused clusters sleep -> everything asleep -> `_skip_asleep` with ~3 islands x 0.5 ms per 4 ms sample = minutes per tubule instead of days. Coasts/boosts: clusters at 0.90-0.999, and clusters at the cap under unloading or flat loads (side-wall grains during the drag decay, aft-face piles during thrust growth) sleep immediately; clusters at the cap under loading (creeping piles) stay in the DEM, so those phases drop from 6000-10000 s per flight-second to the share of the creeping and genuinely moving clusters (estimate 2-4x in those phases). If creep remains dominant, Phase 2 (not in this design): a quasi-static return-mapping island (the same Newton loop with the **real** kernel in `apply_displacement`, which is exactly the kernel's radial return), with inertia/dashpot bounds from the quasi-static velocity du/H and a step-halving estimate of the increment error, verified by `cap_sleep_pile` (c).
Risks: the between-sample nonlinearity bound (kappa_lin) is an estimate of the same kind as `accel_bound`'s, not a proof (check 4 measures it); Newton with the elastic tangent may not converge for clusters with sliding contacts near pull-off (then wake: no silent error); dense |K^-1| memory (1.2 MB at 64 grains) on a memory-short laptop; a cluster thrashing at the cap under loading costs ~ms per step in Jacobians (no regression beyond that); the per-step quasi-static drift makes `_watch` rebuild its tree each step (cheap).

## Entry criterion (summary)

Tier 1 (after the existing rr.max <= 0.90 test fails): no fast window or breakpoint in [T, T+H]; Newton equilibrium with the lifted-cap kernel to |Fext + Fnet| <= 1e-6 * min cap in <= 4 iterations; exact ratios rho_k (pull-off, sliding, rolling, twisting, yield) <= 1 + 1e-6 at T, T+H/2, T+H, each sample evaluated exactly (Newton on a copy), plus the interval term kappa_k (load curvature mapped through |J||K^-1|, and the doubled second difference of rho over the three samples); residual motion: ellipsoid bound delta_k = sqrt(2E (J Ks^-1 J^T)_kk) plus the dashpot term of each cap <= 1e-3 * cap_k for every contact with rho_k > 0.90 (1 % of the remaining margin for the others); no over-cap twist with an exactly zero twist increment; cluster size <= 64.

## Stay and wake (summary)

Stay (every outer step in _step_once and every dts sample in _skip_asleep): recompute the exact ratios at T, T+H/2, T+H from the committed reference by linear prediction + lifted-cap apply_displacement + state() + Newton to tolerance (refactorising K, J only when the residual exceeds it), require rho_k <= 1 + 1e-6 including kappa_k; keep the T+H state pending, use max |du_pred| as the island's motion bound, commit after _verify passes (immediately in _skip_asleep). Wake the whole island into the DEM when: a fast window or breakpoint is in the step; any rho_k > 1 + 1e-6; Newton does not reach tolerance; the twist-quirk guard fires; a DEM grain comes within 2 d_cap (_watch). Wake placement: committed state + linear response to the current load applied with the real kernel (any clip = the flow the DEM would do then, counted), at rest.

## What is exact and what is bounded (summary)

Per sleeping interval and contact k, the DEM's plastic variables could differ from the frozen ones by at most Delta_p_k = delta_k / k_k + Sum_steps max(0, rho_k - 1) cap_k / k_k, where delta_k = sqrt(2E (J Ks^-1 J^T)_kk) + dashpot term (E = kinetic + elastic residual energy at entry; one excursion over the cap is the most a decaying residual can ratchet, since the first clip moves the spring's mean by the excursion) and the second term is the tolerated excess (<= 1e-6 cap/k per step, zero when the prediction is <= 1). The equilibrium forces are exact kernel values up to the measured Newton residual (effect on any ratio <= (|J||K^-1||r|)_k / cap_k <= 1e-6); between samples the ratio is bounded by the larger end value plus kappa_k (load curvature m H^2 |a''|/8 doubled, through |J||K^-1|, ~4e-7 of the cap in the drag decay, 0 for linear ramps; plus the doubled second difference of rho over the three samples, an estimate of the same kind as Loads.accel_bound). Sizes: 57-grain pile at 2e-9 m/s: E <= 1.3e-25 J, delta ~ 4.7e-13 N = 3.6e-5 cap, Delta_p ~ 5.5e-13 m = 3.5e-5 xi_crit (15.6 nm); microgravity clusters: < 1e-9 xi_crit; tolerated flow over the 50 s of boost/coast <= 2e-10 m = 0.013 xi_crit (bound, zero if no excess is predicted). Grains at 1e-4 m/s give delta ~ 1e-6 N >> cap and are refused. All terms are accumulated in n_events (cap_ratchet_bound_m, cap_flow_bound_m, cap_wake_flow_m, cap_relin) and reported in units of xi_crit.

## Risks stated

- Clusters at the cap under *loading* (creeping piles during thrust growth on side walls, drag decay on forward faces) are correctly refused and stay in the DEM; if they dominate the boost/coast cost, a Phase-2 quasi-static return-mapping island (same Newton loop with the real kernel) is needed.
- The between-sample nonlinearity term kappa_lin (doubled second difference of rho over three exact samples) is an estimate of the same kind as Loads.accel_bound's curvature margin, not a proof; check_cap_interval_bound measures it on the real loads.
- Newton with the elastic tangent may fail to converge for clusters with contacts near pull-off or sliding (JKR tension branch); the island then wakes (no silent error) but gains nothing.
- Dense |K^-1| (1.2 MB per 64-grain island) and per-step predict/commit copies add memory and ~0.5 ms per island per sample on a memory-short laptop; cap_sleep_max_grains bounds it.
- Per-step quasi-static drift of tier-1 islands invalidates the _watch tree cache every step (rebuild cost small) and requires the new finite motion bound s[mem] in _verify; a mistake there would be caught by check 10/13-style all-DEM comparisons.
- The residual ratchet bound assumes the residual decays (dashpots present, q_roll/q_twist finite); with q = 0 (undamped tests) the one-excursion argument still holds for a conserved E but should be stated as such in the check.
