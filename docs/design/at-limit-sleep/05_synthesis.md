# Final design: letting static at-limit clusters sleep

Read-only review of `dolle_sim/engine.py`, `statics.py`, `dem.py`, `loads.py`, `verify_full.py`, `tests/test_full_engine.py` at the given paths. Nothing edited, no simulator process started.

## 0. What the code does (verified) and why the measured clusters are refused

1. **Kernel at a cap** (`dem.contact_force` 300-390). Sliding/rolling: `xi = rot(xi) + v dt; f = -k xi - g v; if |f| > cap: xi = -(s f + g v)/k, f *= s`, `cap = mu*Fncrit`, `Fncrit = |Fne + 2 Fpull|`. Strict `>`: at zero relative motion and a constant `Fncrit` nothing changes; with a shrinking `Fncrit` the spring is clipped every step (flow without motion); with a growing elastic demand it is clipped by the excess (creep); the dashpot shifts the clipped history by `g|v|/k`. `apply_displacement -> _advance` (TAU = 1e3 s) is exactly one step of this return map for a finite displacement (design 2 is right). Twisting: `sg = sign(mt)` from the RATE, so at `mt == 0` exactly an over-cap twist is reset to 0 (`tq = 0`), and at a tiny opposite-sign rate it flips (design 2's reading is correct; designs 1 and 3 only note the reset). LAMMPS-conformant: the kernel stays.
2. **Statics with the real `prm`** (`statics.py`): `state()` evaluates the kernel at `dU = 0` (zero rate), so an over-cap twist reads as 0 (ratio 0, not > 1) and clipped sliding/rolling read as exactly the cap; `_jac_coo` (EPS_X = 1e-13 m, i.e. 1e-13 N*m... a force step `k_r EPS_X = 8.5e-14 N ~ 6e-6 Frcrit`) straddles the kink of a contact the DEM left within `g|v|/k ~ 1e-12..1e-8` of its cap, so K and J there are the average of the elastic and the released slope (designs 1 and 3 are right; the existing checks never see it because such islands are refused). `mu` enters the kernel only through the three caps, so with `P_MUT = P_MUR = P_MUTW = 1e300` the kernel is bit-identical whenever no clip branch is taken (all present tier-0 islands).
3. **Entry/stay** (`_try_sleep_island` 1905, `_step_once` 495-505, `_skip_asleep` 1153): entry `rr.max <= 0.90`, stay `<= 0.98` on the linear response `lam_ref + J K^-1 dF`; the residual test is relative to the remaining margin (`0.01*(1-rr)*mu*Fncrit`), zero at a cap. Therefore: the microgravity edge grain (twist 0.962/0.956) and bridge (roll 0.926) fail only the entry hysteresis (design 3's point); the boost/coast piles at ratio 0.999 can never pass, and most of them are not even static: 2e-9 m/s is exactly the creep rate of a rolling spring at its cap under the spin-up's demand growth (`2 Omega_dot/Omega ~ 0.1/s` -> 4e-4 of ~16 nm per 4 ms step = 1.6e-9 m/s). Speeds of 1e-13 m/s are the truly static ones (round-off: 4e-16 m per step).
4. **Redo safety**: `_snapshot` copies `_islands` shallowly (`dict(self._islands)`), so island dicts are shared with the snapshot; any in-place mutation inside `_step_once` would survive `_restore`. `__getstate__` drops only `lu`; `_island_lu` rebuilds it. `_watch` caches the sleeper tree on positions; `_capture_times`, `_early_exit` and `_slv_obstacles` take ASLEEP grains as fixed (`s = 0`).
5. **Flow-time structure** (`loads.py`, `flight.py`): `Omega` is piecewise linear with knots at the breakpoints (so `Omega^2` is exactly quadratic within a step), the profile's thrust/coast pieces are constant, the ascent drag is smooth, the chute/splash pieces are half-sines bounded by breakpoints; `accel_bound` bounds curvature by doubled 5-sample second differences (the project's accepted construction).

## 1. Principle

Two regimes of a sleeping island, decided every step from the same solve, always with the island's committed state being an exact equilibrium of the loads at the step start (one new solve per step, since the start sample is the committed state):

* **Static (exact)**: the elastic (caps-lifted) equilibrium at the step end keeps every sliding/rolling/twisting demand within its cap along the whole step (quasi-convexity along the load chord + a curvature bound) and pull-off/yield/break keep the margin. Then the real kernel takes no clip branch, no plastic variable changes, and the committed state is the DEM's quasi-static state. This covers microgravity, the coast (constant spin, decaying tangential drag = elastic unloading) and every cluster at 0.90-0.98.
* **Creep (bounded)**: if the elastic prediction exceeds a cap (demand growing, or `Fncrit` shrinking), the island is advanced quasi-statically with the kernel's own return map (`apply_displacement` with the real `prm`) iterated to equilibrium, its motion `|du|` entering every bound as a finite displacement, and the step's error estimated by step halving. This covers the burns (rising spin) and the early coast's cap shrink. It is the same kind of reduced model as `slaved.py` (quasi-static stiff modes) and is verified the same way (checks 14-16).

Everything else wakes the island into the DEM (fast loads, breakpoints, pull-off margin, break, yield, no convergence, fast motion, large flow, twist-quirk ambiguity, approach of a DEM grain).

## 2. statics.py additions (pure functions, each unit-tested)

* `lift_caps(prm) -> prm_el`: copy with `P_MUT, P_MUR, P_MUTW = 1e300`.
* `apply_displacement(x, ci, cj, prm, hist, L, dU, with_out=False)`: unchanged behaviour; optionally returns the kernel `out` too.
* `overlaps(x, ci, cj, prm, L) -> delta (nc,)`: `rad_i + rad_j - |x_i - x_j|`, wall: `rad - gap` (for the yield test `delta <= P_DY`).
* `caps(lam, prm, out) -> (Fs, Fr, Mt) (nc, 3)`: `mu_* |Fne + 2 Fpull|` with `Fne = lam[8k]`, `Fpull = out[:,19]`; `kcomp(prm, out) -> (ks, kr, ktw)`: `ks = P_KT*a0` (Mindlin: `P_KT*h[H_A]`; yielded: the flattened a0 as the kernel), `kr = P_KR`, `ktw = P_KTW`.
* `ratios_by_kind(lam, prm, out) -> (r_pull, r_slide, r_roll, r_twist)` (the four arrays `margins()` reduces); `margins` and `margins_bounded` unchanged and always called with the REAL `prm`.
* `snap_to_caps(hist, lam, prm, out, rel=2**-40) -> max_rel_change`: for every sds component with ratio `> 1 - rel`, scale the history (3-vector or scalar, sign kept) to exactly `(1 - rel)*cap/k`. Called on every state handed to the DEM or evaluated by the real kernel at zero rate. Relative force change `<= 9.1e-13` per snap (reported). Prevents the twist reset (and any re-clip) in the DEM's first force evaluation at `v = w = 0`.
* `row_norms(lu, J, block=256) -> rn (8nc,)`: `||K^-T J_k^T||_2` per component via block transposed solves `lu.solve(Jt[:, a:b].toarray(), trans='T')` (as `energy_bounds` does). Then `|(J K^-1 f)_k| <= rn_k ||f||_2` for any force vector f (Cauchy-Schwarz) - no dense `|K^-1|`.
* `solve_equilibrium(x, ci, cj, prm_apply, prm_el, hist, L, lu, Fext, du0, tol, n_max, q_max) -> dict(ok, du, xn, hn, lam, out, Fnet, r, n_it, q, broke)`:
  ```
  du = du0.copy(); r_prev = inf
  for it in range(n_max):
      xn, hn, out = apply_displacement(x, ci, cj, prm_apply, hist, L, du.reshape(-1,6), with_out=True)  # always from the reference state
      lam, Fnet, out = state(xn, ci, cj, prm_el, hn, L)       # caps lifted: reads the springs as they are, no re-clip, no twist reset
      r = Fext + Fnet; rn_ = |r|_inf
      broke = (out[:,10] < 0.5).any(); if broke: return ok=False
      if rn_ <= tol: return ok=True
      if it > 0 and rn_ > q_max * r_prev: return ok=False       # no contraction (released caps leave a mechanism)
      r_prev = rn_; du += lu.solve(r)
  return ok=False
  ```
  With `prm_apply = prm_el` this is Newton on the smooth elastic problem (`n_max = 6`, converges in 1-3 iterations; `du0` = linear prediction). With `prm_apply = prm` (real) the kernel's return map is inside the loop and the fixed point is the equilibrium of the one-shot-returned state (`n_max = 40`, `q_max = 0.9`; contraction factor = released/total stiffness in the released direction: ~1e-4 for pile grains with sliding springs, ~0.5 for an edge grain held by its twist, -> 1 for a mechanism, which is then correctly handed to the DEM).
* `flow_increments(h_ref, h_new, x_ref, x_new, ci, cj, L, prm, out_new) -> dp (nc,3)`: plastic increment per sds component = `|k (h_new - trial)|/k` where `trial` = `rot(h_ref)` + the kinematic increment of `du` (recomputed by one lifted-cap `apply_displacement`): i.e. `dp = |h_el - h_real|` componentwise norm; zero where nothing clipped.
* `twist_ambiguous(h_ref, h_el, prm, caps_new) -> bool mask`: components whose elastic trial twist exceeds the new cap and whose increment `h_el - h_ref` is exactly 0 or of opposite sign to `h_ref` (the kernel's answer there depends on the sign of a ~1e-20 rad/s rate; hand to the DEM).

## 3. loads.py addition

`Loads.accel_curv(T0, T1) -> (3,)`: per-axis bound on `|a''|` of the profile over the interval: doubled second differences of the 5 samples of `accel_bound` (refactor `accel_bound` to call it) and, where `knots_in` reports a knot inside, the slope jump divided by `h` (a kink's second difference). Zero on constant and piecewise-linear pieces, `omega^2 A (1 + 1e-3)` on the half-sines, the usual heuristic for the smooth ascent drag (stated in the docstring; measured by check 17). Release and vibration are fast windows and never reach the certificate. Engine side `_island_curv(mem, T0, T1) -> ||dF||_2` with per-grain translational deviation `m_g * (l^2/8) * (||accel_curv|| + 2 Omega_dot^2 |r_perp,g|)`, `l = T1 - T0`, rotations 0 (Euler torque piecewise constant, Coriolis 0 at rest).

Pending end states live in `self._isl_pending` (popped by `__getstate__`), never in the dict. Islands from old pickles/autosaves lack `tier`: `_island_upgrade(isl)` sets `prm_el`, recomputes `K, J, lu, lam, out` with the lifted kernel at the stored state (bit-identical for them, they are below 0.90), `rn`, `k`, `tier = 1`, zero `bnd`. The README's bit-for-bit statement is scoped to one code version.

### 4.2 `_island_advance(isl, T0, T1) -> (ok, new_isl, why)` - the certificate and the step
Preconditions handled by the caller: no fast window / breakpoint in `[T0, T1]`, the committed state (positions in `self.x[mem]`, histories in `self._hist[rows]`) is the exact equilibrium of `F(T0) = isl["F_ref"]` to `tol`.
1. `tol = island_tol_rel * min_k cap_k` (default 1e-6). `F1 = _island_load(mem, T1)`, `du0 = lu.solve(F1 - F_ref)`.
2. **Elastic solve** `E = solve_equilibrium(prm_apply=prm_el, n_max=6)`. If `E.broke` -> wake `break`. If not ok -> wake `newton`.
3. **Deviation bound** `D (8nc)`: `D_k = rn_k * (||dF_curv||_2 + ||E.r||_2) + 2 |lam_1 - (lam_ref + J du)|_k + rn_k ||f_dyn||_2`, with `f_dyn` the per-grain vector `m |du|/l^2 + g_max |du|/l` (`g_max` = largest dashpot coefficient of the island's contacts: `max(dpre, x_gt dpre, gr, gtw/R^2)`, `dpre = P_DAMPC sqrt(a)`). Factor 2 on the first-order terms when `n_since_refresh > 0` (stale K/J). Record `bnd.newton_rel = max(rn ||r||/cap)`, `bnd.dyn_rel`, `bnd.curv_m = max(rn ||dF_curv||/k)`.
4. **Static test**: `w0 = margins_bounded(lam_ref, D)`, `w1 = margins_bounded(lam_1, D)` by kind. Pull-off: `max(w0, w1)_pull <= 1 - margin` else wake `pulloff`. Yield: `overlaps(x_1) <= P_DY` else wake `yield`. If every sds component has `max(w0, w1) <= 1 + island_tol_rel`: **tier 1 passes**. Justification: the linear response to the chord load is affine in t, `|affine|/(affine > 0)` is quasi-convex, so its maximum over the step is at an end; the exact response differs from it by the load curvature (`rn ||dF_curv||`), the kernel nonlinearity (measured at the end, doubled) and the dynamics of the drift (`f_dyn`), all in D. Pending state: `x_1, h_1 (= E.hn, elastic), lam_1, out_1, F_ref = F1, T_ref = T1, du_max = |du|_inf, tier 1`; `bnd.flow_tol_m += max(0, max_k(w - 1)) * xi_cap` (zero whenever the prediction is <= 1). Cost: one solve.
5. **Creep** (only if `creep_islands` and the failing components are sds caps only): `C1 = solve_equilibrium(prm_apply=prm, n_max=40, q_max=0.9, du0=E.du)` to `F1`; then the half-step chain `Cm` from the committed state to `F(Tmid)`, `C2` from `Cm`'s state to `F1` (same `lu`). Wake `newton` if any fails. Checks on `C2`: `|du|_inf <= island_motion_m` (1e-10 m) else `motion`; plastic increment `dp_k <= island_flow_max * xi_cap,k` (0.05) else `flow`; `twist_ambiguous` empty else `twist`; pull-off/yield/break as in 4 with `lam` from `C2`; **path error** `e_k = 2 |h_C1 - h_C2|_k / xi_cap,k <= island_path_tol` (1e-3) else `path` (Richardson for a first-order method; the one-shot return differs from the continuous one at second order in the tangential increment, ~1e-9 of `xi_cap` per step for 1e-12 m drifts; the half-step pair measures it). Pending state = `C2`'s (`x, h, lam, out, F1, T1, du_max`, tier 2); `bnd.path_m += max_k e_k xi_cap,k`, `bnd.flow_m += max_k dp_k`, `bnd.curv_m` as a cumulative flow bound in tier 2 (an in-step load excursion beyond the chord can flow the spring by its excess: `rn ||dF_curv||/k`, summed).
6. `snap_to_caps` on the pending histories (`bnd.snap_rel = max`). Refresh flag: `n_since_refresh >= 1000` or `du_since_refresh > 1e-11 m` or `n_it > 3` -> recompute `K, J, lu, rn` at commit (`island_refactor`).
Reasons are counted as `n_events["island_wake_" + why]`; `island_static_steps`, `island_creep_steps`, `island_newton_iters` counted.

### 4.3 Entry `_try_sleep_island(mem, touch, T, H)` (replaces the body from `jacobians` on)
E0. As now: `H > 0`, no fast window/breakpoint in `[T, T+H]`, rows, size `<= max_island`; `prm_el`; `K, J = jacobians(..., prm_el, ...)`; `lu = splu(K)` (refuse on failure).
E1. `lam0, Fnet0, out0 = state(x, ..., prm_el, hist)`; `du0 = lu.solve(F(T) + Fnet0)`; `E = solve_equilibrium(prm_el, n_max 6)`. If ok and every sds ratio of `lam_E <= 1 + tol`: the elastic equilibrium is the state (tier 1 candidate). Else if `creep_islands`: `solve_equilibrium(prm, 40, 0.9)` (the DEM state is flowing; the return map places it) -> tier 2 candidate; refuse `cluster_awake_newton` / `over_limit` otherwise. `du` = the displacement from the DEM state to the placed state.
E2. Pull-off `<= 1 - margin` at the placed state and at T+H (from E's `lu`), overlaps `<= P_DY`, no break.
E3. **Residual** `E_res = Ek + 0.5 du^T K du` (K lifted, >= the real stiffness as a quadratic form, so an upper bound on the recoverable energy; add `|dF_step| |K^-1 dF_step|`, the energy the step's load change can pump in). Ratchet bound per sds component: `dxi_k = E_res/cap_k + g_k v_E/k_k <= island_ratchet_rel * xi_cap,k` (1e-3), `v_E = sqrt(2 E_res/m_min)` (sliding), `R_max sqrt(2 E_res/I_min)` (rolling), `sqrt(2 E_res/I_min)` (twist rate). Justification: plastic work at a capped spring is `cap * |dxi_p|` and total dissipation cannot exceed the available energy; the dashpot term is the most a clip at the residual's speed can shift the history. One-time per entry, recorded `bnd.ratchet_m`. Pull-off with the residual: `dl = energy_bounds(K, J, E_res)`; `margins_bounded(lam, dl)_pull <= 1 - margin`. If the ratchet bound fails and no sds component is above `1 - margin` (with `dl`): `_linres` as now (its K, J are the lifted ones, identical there); else refuse `cluster_awake_ratchet`.
E4. Place: `self.x[mem], self._hist[rows] = snapped placed state`, `v = w = 0`; recompute `K, J, lu, lam, out` there (as now), `rn`, `k`; build the dict; then the first certificate `_island_advance(isl, T, T+H)` must pass (else undo the placement? No: the placement is an exact equilibrium of the current load and at rest, a valid DEM state; keep it in the DEM and refuse `cluster_awake_certificate`). **Anti-chatter** (tier 1 only): with the same linear response, the extrapolated sds ratios at `min(T + hyst_steps*H, next breakpoint)` (`island_hyst_steps` = 20) must stay `<= 1 + tol`, else refuse `cluster_awake_drift` (a sleep must be worth 20 steps; tier 2 needs no hysteresis, it only wakes on real failures).
Counters: `cluster_sleep` (as now), `cluster_sleep_cap` (any sds ratio `>= 1 - margin` at entry), `cluster_sleep_creep` (tier 2 at entry).

## 5. Exact / bounded
* **Exact** (tier 1): the plastic variables (no clip branch is ever taken while the certificate holds); the committed state is the kernel's equilibrium to `tol`; loads evaluated exactly at the samples. Approximations stated: Newton residual (`<= 1e-6 cap`, not cumulative), neglected dynamics of the quasi-static drift (`<= rn ||f_dyn||`, ~1e-9 cap at 1e-13 m per step, required `<= 1e-6`), the dropped entry residual (ratchet `<= 1e-3 xi_cap` by construction; ~1e-17 m = 6e-10 xi_crit for the 57-grain pile at 2e-9 m/s), the tolerated excess (`<= 1e-6 cap/k` per step, zero whenever the prediction is `<= 1`), snapping `<= 9.1e-13` relative. The curvature/nonlinearity terms are certificate inputs, not errors: if they hold there is no flow; their rigor is that of `accel_bound` (exact for constant, linear and half-sine pieces; heuristic for the smooth drag; checked by 17).
* **Bounded** (tier 2): the flow amounts are the kernel's own return map; the one-shot vs continuous return and the in-step path are estimated by the half-step pair (`2 |h_1step - h_2step|`, first-order method, `<= 1e-3 xi_cap` per step, summed and reported) plus the load-curvature flow (`rn ||dF_curv||/k`, ~2e-7 cap per step in the drag decay, 0 in the burns' linear spin-up... `Omega^2` quadratic: `2 Omega_dot^2 r m l^2/8 ~ 3e-16 N = 2e-8 cap`), dynamics `<= 1e-6 cap` per step, Newton `<= 1e-6 cap`. Totals for the measured cases: tier-1 islands (coast, microgravity) `< 1e-6 cap` plus the one-time ratchet; a creeping pile over a 12 s burn (3000 steps) `<= 3000 * 1e-3 xi_cap` as the worst allowed, typically `~1e-5 xi_cap` measured per step by the half-step pair (reported as `island_bound_xi`).

## 6. Verification (verify_full.py checks 14-17; CHECKS.md via tools/checks_table.py)

## 8. Flags on the designs (judged against the code)
* Design 1: evaluates three exact samples per step (two are redundant once the start state is committed); the "doubled second difference of rho" between samples is a heuristic (dropped here: the start/end quasi-convexity with the loads' curvature covers it); storing dense `|K^-1|` is unnecessary (row norms); its twist guard acts only at entry, but the DEM's first `compute_forces` after any wake runs at `v = w = 0` (`mt == 0`), so every state handed to the DEM must be snapped; offering single-wall grains to `_try_sleep_island` is pointless (a single contact at its cap is a mechanism, correctly ROLL); it refuses all creeping piles (the burns stay at 1e8 DEM steps/s).
* Design 2: correct kernel reading (return map, rate-sign quirk) and the cleanest commit/pending logic; but its analytic bounds for one-shot-vs-continuous return and dynamic lag (sections 2.2, 2.4) are long derivations that the half-step pair measures directly; its `C, G, Md` per island at entry cost another 12 kernel sweeps per DOF; `qs_bound_cum_rel` conflates one-time and cumulative terms; it drops the pull-off hysteresis to `1 - margin` at entry (kept at `1 - margin` here too, but with the residual ellipsoid included).
* Design 3: right about the entry hysteresis, the margin-relative residual test, the kink, the twist reset and the `_snapshot` sharing; its budgeted flow (`B_STEP = 2e-4` per step, `B_TOTAL`, released-compliance norm `beta`) tolerates unresolved flow in a frozen island and needs the Schur-block machinery to bound its amplification - replaced by the strict `1 + 1e-6` test plus the creep step, which resolves the flow instead of bounding it; its Tier 2 (Woodbury active-set Newton) is more than needed (the fixed-point iteration with the elastic K converges whenever the island is not a mechanism, and a mechanism belongs to the DEM); `k_s` and `k_tw` numbers differ 2x between designs (unimportant: all bounds are relative).
* Common: `statics.state()` with the real prm misreads over-cap twists as 0 today (latent; a capped twist cluster never entered). None of the designs noted that `_capture_times`/`_early_exit`/`_slv_obstacles` must see the island drift `du_max` (design 2 did for two of them).

## 9. Expected speed-up
Microgravity (T+88-313 s and the fall to T+460 s): the edge grain and the bridge sleep (entry = stay), everything asleep -> `_skip_asleep` 2 s per call, ~3 islands x 500 samples x ~0.5 ms = minutes per tubule instead of days. Coast (constant spin, decaying drag): piles under unloading sleep as tier 1 (one solve per step, ~0.3 ms for 150 contacts), the cap-shrink of aft contacts creeps as tier 2 (~3 ms per step): the coast drops from 6000-10000 s per flight-second to the DEM cost of genuinely moving grains (the 1e-4 m/s movers: slaved/rolling models or the DEM). Burns: creeping piles at ~3-5 ms per step (~1 s per flight-second for a 57-grain pile) instead of ~4e5 DEM steps x 57 grains. Campaign: weeks -> days or less.

## Entry criterion (summary)

Replace `rr.max <= 1 - 5*margin` by: (E0) no fast window or breakpoint in [T, T+H], size <= max_island, K_el = jacobians with caps lifted (P_MUT = P_MUR = P_MUTW = 1e300) factorises; (E1) exact equilibrium of F(T) by solve_equilibrium from the DEM state: first with the lifted kernel (Newton, <= 6 iterations, |r|_inf <= tol = 1e-6 min cap); if every sliding/rolling/twisting ratio of that state is <= 1 + 1e-6 it is the placed state (tier 1), else (creep_islands on) the same loop with the real kernel inside (the return map, <= 40 iterations, contraction q <= 0.9) places it (tier 2); otherwise refuse (cluster_awake_newton / over_limit); (E2) pull-off ratio <= 1 - margin at T and T+H (residual ellipsoid dl = energy_bounds(K_el, J_el, E_res) included), overlaps <= P_DY, no break; (E3) residual E_res = Ek + du^T K_el du/2 + |dF_step||K_el^-1 dF_step| must satisfy the ratchet bound at every sds component E_res/cap_k + g_k v_E/k_k <= 1e-3 xi_cap,k (plastic work <= available energy; dashpot shift of a clip), else _linres as now when no sds component is within 1 - margin of its cap, else refuse cluster_awake_ratchet; (E4) after placing (snapped to caps, v = w = 0) the first certificate _island_advance(isl, T, T+H) passes, and for tier 1 the linearly extrapolated sds ratios over 20 steps (or to the next breakpoint) stay <= 1 + 1e-6 (anti-chatter, cluster_awake_drift). Single grains on one wall: entry = stay (<= 1 - margin with the curvature term) with the same 20-step hysteresis; a single contact at its cap is a mechanism and goes to ROLL as now.

## Stay and wake (summary)

Stay (every outer step in _step_once; every dts sample in _skip_asleep, islands in lockstep): from the committed state (exact equilibrium of F(T0), positions/histories live in self.x/self._hist) one lifted-cap Newton solve to F(T1) gives the elastic end state; deviation bound D_k = rn_k (||dF_curv|| + ||r|| + ||f_dyn||) + 2|lam_1 - lam_lin,1|_k (rn = row norms of J K^-1; dF_curv from Loads.accel_curv and 2 Omega_dot^2 r over l = T1 - T0; f_dyn = m|du|/l^2 + g_max|du|/l); worst = max(margins_bounded(lam_ref, D), margins_bounded(lam_1, D)) (quasi-convex along the chord, so the ends bound the step). Tier 1 (static, exact): every sds component <= 1 + 1e-6, pull-off <= 1 - margin, overlaps <= P_DY, no break -> pending end state, du_max = |du|_inf. Else, if only sds caps are exceeded and creep_islands: real-kernel return-map solve to F(T1) plus the half-step pair (T0 -> Tmid -> T1): converged (<= 40 it, q <= 0.9), |du|_inf <= 1e-10 m, plastic increment <= 5% xi_cap per component, no twist with zero/opposite-sign increment over its cap, pull-off/yield/break as above, path error 2|h_1step - h_2step| <= 1e-3 xi_cap -> pending = the half-step state (tier 2), bounds accumulated. Pending states are committed in step() after _verify passes (replace the dict; write x/hist), immediately in _skip_asleep; a redo discards them. The DEM sees the island at its committed start state with s[members] = du_max in every bound (_watch lim, _capture_times, _early_exit, _slv_obstacles). Wake (whole island into the DEM, reason counted island_wake_*): fast window or breakpoint in the step; pulloff; break; yield; newton (no convergence = mechanism); loading (sds over cap with creep off); motion; flow; twist; path; approach (_watch, a DEM grain within 2 d_cap); linres islands keep the present 0.98 linear test. Placement at wake: at the step start the committed state itself (snapped, at rest, + the linres residual as now); mid-step the same solve from the committed state to F(T_w), fallback to the present linear-response placement (counted).

## What is exact and what is bounded (summary)

Tier 1 (no predicted flow) is exact in the plastic variables: while the bounded ratio stays <= 1 at every sliding/rolling/twisting component the kernel takes no clip branch, so the committed state equals the DEM's quasi-static state up to (i) the Newton residual, effect <= rn_k ||r|| <= 1e-6 cap per step, not cumulative; (ii) the neglected dynamics of the quasi-static drift, <= rn_k ||m du/l^2 + g_max du/l|| (~1e-9 cap at the measured 1e-13 m per step, required <= 1e-6); (iii) the tolerated excess <= 1e-6 cap/k per step (summed, zero when the prediction is <= 1); (iv) snapping to (1 - 2^-40) cap, <= 9.1e-13 relative per snap; (v) the dropped entry residual: plastic work <= available energy gives |dxi_p| <= E_res/cap + g v_E/k <= 1e-3 xi_cap by construction, one-time per entry (57-grain pile at 2e-9 m/s: E_res ~ 1.3e-25 J -> 1e-17 m = 6e-10 xi_crit; grains at 1e-4 m/s give 4e-10 m and are refused). The between-sample inputs (load curvature rn||dF_curv||, ~2e-7 cap per 4 ms step in the drag decay, 2e-8 in the spin-up, 0 on constant/linear pieces; the kernel nonlinearity measured at the end and doubled) are certificate conditions with the rigor of Loads.accel_bound (exact for constant, piecewise-linear and half-sine pieces, the project's accepted heuristic for the smooth drag), verified by check 17. Tier 2 (creep) is bounded: flow amounts are the kernel's own return map; one-shot-vs-continuous return and in-step path dependence are estimated per step by the half-step pair e_k = 2|h_1step - h_2step|_k <= 1e-3 xi_cap (first-order Richardson; expected ~1e-5 xi_cap per step for 1e-12 m drifts), summed and reported; load-curvature flow rn||dF_curv||/k per step summed; dynamics and Newton as in tier 1; |du| <= 1e-10 m per step enters every bound exactly. Reported per island and in n_events: island_bound_xi = max over islands of (flow_tol_m + ratchet_m + path_m + curv_m)/xi_crit, island_flow_m, island_snap_rel, island_wake_transient_m (the DEM's measured motion of woken grains). Worst allowed over a 12 s burn (3000 creep steps): 3 xi_crit if every step saturated the tolerance; the measured values (check 16) are expected ~1e-2 xi_crit.

## Risks stated

- Creep islands are a reduced quasi-static model (like slaved.py); their per-step error is estimated by the half-step pair (Richardson for a first-order return map), not proven; check 16 against the all-DEM at two steps is what certifies it, and creep_islands stays off until it passes
- The between-sample curvature term inherits accel_bound's doubled-second-difference heuristic for the smooth ascent drag; exact for constant, linear and half-sine pieces; check 17 measures violations on the real loads
- The return-map fixed-point iteration with the elastic K converges slowly (q ~ 0.5-0.9) for clusters held only by twist/rolling springs and fails for mechanisms: those wake into the DEM each step (status-quo cost for them); a consistent elasto-plastic tangent would be a later improvement
- Islands now drift (du_max <= 1e-10 m per step) and their committed positions change every step: every place that took ASLEEP as s = 0 (_watch, _capture_times, _early_exit, _slv_obstacles) must be updated; a missed one would be an unverified 1e-3 d_cap assumption (the _missed safety net still catches contact formation)
- The all-DEM reference at a cap is itself noisy (its own residual ratchets, round-off clipping), so verification differences at the ~1e-5 cap level are dominated by the DEM's step noise; the criteria include the step error and the reported bound
- Twist caps with a shrinking Fncrit under exactly zero relative twist rate are rate-sign ambiguous in the kernel (LAMMPS rule); the engine hands them to the DEM (island_wake_twist), whose answer there depends on a ~1e-20 rad/s noise sign; a kernel change is out of scope
- Campaign autosaves continue under the new rule but are no longer bit-for-bit the uninterrupted old run; the README statement must be scoped to one code version, and bound accumulators start at the resume point
- Per-step cost grows by one lifted solve per island (~0.3 ms at 150 contacts) and ~3 solves for creep islands; islands near max_island = 800 grains would need the exact-evaluation path capped (fall back to the present linear 0.98 test above ~128 grains)
- If the genuinely moving grains (1e-4 m/s) stay in the DEM at 5-18 ns the coast still costs hundreds of seconds per flight-second; the speed-up then depends on the slaved/rolling models taking them
