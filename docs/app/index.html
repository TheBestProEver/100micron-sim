Dust-collision simulator

Choose the dust and press Run. Hundreds of grains float, spin and collide in a simulated chamber, and you get the speed below which they stick. It all runs in your browser.

Getting the simulator ready

The physics engine runs inside your browser, so it has to download first. This takes about 15–30 s the first time and a few seconds afterwards.

  1. Download the Python runtime (≈10 MB)
  2. Load the maths libraries (numpy, scipy)
  3. Load the DOLL-E simulator
  4. Warm up

Will the dust come off the bottom of the tubules?

Each grain sticks to the glass with a pull-off force of 1.5πwR. Below, that force divided by the grain's weight on Earth gives the "threshold" in g, spread over the grain sizes and the scatter in stickiness. It is compared with the loads in each phase, at the tubule corner farthest from the spin axis. Edit the tubules and recompute (takes about a second).

Cross-check with REBOUND

REBOUND is a widely used N-body code. None of DOLL-E comes from it; it is used only for this comparison (these are stored desktop results, because REBOUND does not run in browsers). REBOUND moves the grains with its leapfrog integrator and its collision detection; our sticking model, our exact drag + Coriolis velocity step and our wall handling run inside it. So this checks the motion and collision bookkeeping, not the sticking physics. Over the first 0.2 s, before any collision, REBOUND's high-accuracy IAS15 integrator and ours agree to under a nanometre for the same equations of motion.

Cross-check with LAMMPS

LAMMPS is a widely used open-source particle simulator with its own, independent implementation of the same adhesive (JKR) contact model. None of DOLL-E comes from it; it is used only for this comparison (stored desktop results). LAMMPS ends a contact at the maximum pulling force, DOLL-E at the end of the stable contact curve; DOLL-E can use either, so the comparison uses LAMMPS's rule. Collisions are compared without damping: LAMMPS refuses the damping law DOLL-E uses (Tsuji's) with this contact model, and the ones it allows are different. Differences are LAMMPS minus DOLL-E.
